MEV TRACK CONCEPT 01 - CAD APPEARANCE STUDY

Purpose
Translate the selected Track styling direction into solid CAD exterior geometry
around the original MEV assembly. This is a styling review model, not a release
for printing, molding, or machining.

Deliverables
MEV_Track_Concept_01.step: complete assembly with the redesigned exterior.
Track_Exterior_Only.step: new exterior components in the original coordinates.
track_front.png, track_rear.png, track_side.png: renders of actual CAD geometry.
track_build_report.json: dimensions, component replacement list and build checks.
track_verification.json: independent verification of the exported assembly.

Scope
Replace the original 11 exterior sections and 4 outer bumper sections.
Keep original chassis, drivetrain, batteries, steering, suspension, wheels,
body-support posts, and camera/antenna components in their existing positions.
The tall original body-support posts remain visible. Their final length and
fastening method need a separate design decision before manufacturing.

Styling
Sculpted GT nose; broad wheel arches; coupe cabin; dark window graphics;
black side-intake panels; front splitter; ribbed rear trim; raised split wing.
The wing has a central gap for the original antenna.
Window, lamp and vent graphics are colored solid panels for appearance review.
They do not represent installed lamps, actual glass, or verified cooling ducts.

Geometry parameters
Wheelbase: approximately 209.4 mm.
Wheel-opening cutter radius: 39 mm versus approximately 33.5 mm tire radius.
Shell loft inset: 2.2 mm. This is an approximate shell construction, not a
guarantee of uniform wall thickness normal to every curved surface.
Body-post clearance holes: 8 mm diameter, concept-level placement.

Engineering limits
Static solid overlap checks do not prove running clearance.
Steering sweep, suspension travel, assembly tolerances, mounting brackets,
fasteners, access for maintenance, printability and aerodynamic performance
are not certified. Mounting holes alone are not a completed mounting design.
The CAD shell is an interpretation of the reference image, not an exact
reconstruction of that image or a dimensioned production design.
Headlight revision: tapered dark housings and narrow white LED styling strips replace the blue panels. Exterior now contains 45 solids.
Uniform blue finish: MEV_Track_Blue_01.step and Track_Exterior_Blue_01.step. Color-only changes preserve all geometry. Website exploded animation is display-only; CAD remains assembled.
